A Breakout-style game. The paddle follows your gaze, read from a webcam by an on-device face model, downloaded once and cached.

Pressing this asks your browser for camera access. The video frame is read directly by a face model that runs on your device. No frame or landmark is ever transmitted — the page-level policy above refuses any request that isn't to this same site, so nothing here has anywhere else to send it.

Also downloads the face model, served from this site and cached by your browser afterwards.

Calibrate your gaze

Look at the left edge of your screen and press "Capture left". Then look at the right edge and press "Capture right". This maps your own comfortable range of eye movement to the full width of the paddle track.
