Chief

A flyable, procedural scene built live from three public feeds — Hacker News, GitHub's public events and the NVD's CVE feed — as one way of showing what staying current with technology looks like, continuously, in the background.

Also try Echo and Iris, which read a person instead of the news.

Pressing this starts three independent, ongoing requests: to Hacker News, to GitHub, and to the NVD. Each is a plain, unauthenticated GET, repeated on its own schedule for as long as this tab stays open, the same way loading any news site would fetch its own front page. This is the site's equivalent of asking for camera or microphone access, except what's being requested is network access instead of hardware — see specs/F06_CHIEF.md.

Use the arrow keys to look and WASD to move; no mouse or pointer lock required.

The scene
